Questions

Is the score what a real ATS would give me?

No. Every company configures its ATS differently, and none publish their scoring. Passline estimates how well your resume matches a job description and how easily software can parse it. Treat it as a guide for improving your resume, not a guarantee.

Which AI tools work with Passline?

Any tool that supports remote MCP servers over HTTP, including Claude and ChatGPT. Check your tool's documentation for how to add a custom connector.

Does Passline see my conversations?

No. Passline only receives the tool calls your AI makes, such as fetching your active resume or saving a result.

What file types can I upload?

PDF, DOC, DOCX, Markdown and plain text, or you can paste your resume directly. You can review and edit the extracted text to fix any parsing errors.
